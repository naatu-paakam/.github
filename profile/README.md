# Naatu Paakam

**Apps for everyday family life.**

Naatu Paakam ("native kitchen" in Southern India culture) builds simple, focused tools for families — managing the day to day work of families, thier home, the learnings, and the memories in between.

---

## Our Apps

| App | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [**JsDayCare**](https://github.com/naatu-paakam/jsdaycare) | Daycare & school management portal — attendance, rooms, staff, parent invites | 🟢 Active |
| [**One Family**](https://github.com/naatu-paakam/one-family) | Family memory space — stories, events, family tree, AI summaries | 🟢 Live |
| [**Pkeep**](https://github.com/naatu-paakam/pkeep) | Worker & visitor registry for Indian housing societies | 🟢 MVP |
| [**AI Companion**](https://github.com/naatu-paakam/ai-companion) | AI assistant with persistent user contexts | 🟡 MVP |
